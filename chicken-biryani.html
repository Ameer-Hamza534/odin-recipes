Chicken Biryani

A flavorful Pakistani rice dish made with chicken, basmati rice, aromatic spices, and a delicious blend of herbs and yogurt.

Ingredients

  • 500g chicken
  • 2 cups basmati rice
  • 2 onions, sliced
  • 2 tomatoes, chopped
  • 1 cup yogurt
  • 2–3 green chilies
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • ½ cup cooking oil
  • 1 tsp red chili powder
  • ½ tsp turmeric powder
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • 1 tsp coriander powder
  • Salt to taste
  • 2–3 tbsp fresh coriander
  • 2–3 tbsp fresh mint
  • 1–2 tbsp lemon juice
  • 2–3 drops yellow food color (optional)

Steps to make Chicken Biryani

  1. Wash and soak the basmati rice for about 20–30 minutes.
  2. Heat oil in a pot and fry the sliced onions until golden brown.
  3. Remove some fried onions for garnishing.
  4. Add ginger-garlic paste and chicken. Cook for 5–7 minutes.
  5. Add tomatoes, yogurt, green chilies, red chili powder, turmeric, cumin, 6. coriander powder, and salt.
  6. Cook until the chicken is tender and the oil separates from the masala.
  7. Boil the soaked rice separately with salt until about 80% cooked, then drain.
  8. Layer the rice over the chicken masala.
  9. Add mint, coriander, lemon juice, fried onions, and food color.
  10. Cover and cook on low heat for 15–20 minutes.
  11. Gently mix and serve hot.
