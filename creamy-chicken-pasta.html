Creamy Chicken Pasta

A creamy and comforting pasta dish prepared with tender chicken, pasta, garlic, and a rich white sauce. It's quick and easy to make at home.

Ingredients

  • 250g pasta
  • 250g boneless chicken
  • 2 tbsp cooking oil
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 cup milk
  • ½ cup cream
  • ½ cup grated cheese
  • 1 tsp black pepper
  • ½ tsp chili flakes
  • ½ tsp oregano
  • Salt to taste
  • ½ cup sliced capsicum (optional)
  • ½ cup sliced mushrooms (optional)

Steps to Prepare Creamy Chicken Pasta

  1. Boil the pasta in salted water according to the package instructions. Drain and set aside.
  2. Cut the chicken into small pieces.
  3. Heat oil in a pan and cook the chicken until lightly golden.
  4. Add garlic and cook for about 1 minute.
  5. Add butter, milk, and cream. Stir well.
  6. Add salt, black pepper, chili flakes, and oregano.
  7. Add cheese and cook until the sauce becomes creamy.
  8. Add capsicum and mushrooms if desired.
  9. Add the boiled pasta and mix everything together.
  10. Cook for another 2–3 minutes and serve hot.
